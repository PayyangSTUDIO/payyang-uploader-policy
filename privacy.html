Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

PAYYANG STUDIO Asset Uploader is a Discord bot that helps users connect their Roblox accounts and upload supported assets using official Roblox authorization.

Information We Collect

When you connect your Roblox account, we process your Roblox account identifier and OAuth authorization tokens required to provide the requested features.

We also process Discord user identifiers and files submitted through the bot to provide upload and conversion services.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to connect accounts, provide requested asset upload features, process submitted files, and maintain bot security and functionality.

Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens are encrypted before storage. We take reasonable measures to protect stored information from unauthorized access.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with Roblox only as necessary to perform actions explicitly authorized by the user.

Account Disconnection and Data Deletion

Users can disconnect their Roblox account using the bot's disconnect command. Users may contact the service operator to request deletion of stored account-related information.

Third-Party Services

This service uses Discord and Roblox services. Their respective privacy policies and terms also apply.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the PAYYANG STUDIO team through its official Discord server.
